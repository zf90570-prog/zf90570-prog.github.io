Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 26, 2026

OpenClaw Gmail Sender is a personal research automation tool used to send user-authorized research emails through the Gmail API.

Information Accessed

The application may access Google account information and Gmail permissions explicitly authorized by the user through Google OAuth.

How Information Is Used

Google user data is used only to perform user-authorized Gmail operations required by the configured research automation workflow, such as sending research literature reports.

Data Sharing

Google user data is not sold, rented, shared with advertisers, or used for advertising purposes.

Data Storage

OAuth credentials required for the application to function are stored securely for the user's personal automation environment. They are not published or intentionally shared with third parties.

Data Retention and Revocation

The user may revoke the application's Google account access at any time through their Google Account security settings.
