OpenClaw Gmail Sender

OpenClaw Gmail Sender is a personal research automation tool used to send research literature reports and other user-authorized research emails through the Gmail API.

The application is intended for personal research workflow automation and is not offered as a public commercial service.

Google API Usage

Google OAuth is used to authorize Gmail operations required by the user's configured research automation workflows.

Google user data is not sold, rented, or used for advertising.
